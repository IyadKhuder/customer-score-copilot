# POL-004 — Site/Market-Level Exception Rules

Site 1 (established market) follows standard thresholds in POL-002 without modification. Sites onboarded within the last 12 months operate under a "grace period" exception: early-warning thresholds are relaxed by 25% (e.g., a 50-point drop rather than 40) to account for immature scoring calibration and thinner historical baselines. Any site-level exception must be reviewed quarterly and does not apply to bin migrations of 3+ bins, which always trigger standard escalation regardless of site tenure.
