# POL-007 — Escalation and Committee Review Triggers

Cases meeting any of the following go to the monthly credit risk committee rather than being resolved by analyst-level review alone: (a) any account migrating 3+ bins downward in one period, (b) any account with 2+ early-warning triggers in the same period, (c) any site showing an aggregate downward migration rate 50% above its trailing 6-month average (a site-wide, not account-level, signal). Committee output should include the account's or site's trend, the policy basis for escalation, and a recommended action.
