# POL-006 — Score Recovery and Reinstatement

**Version:** v1.1 experiment (synthetic project), 2026-10-10. Not approved for production use. The original recovery rule remains unchanged; scope and decision boundaries are working clarifications.

## Recovery and reinstatement rule (unchanged)

An account that has stabilized in an improved bin for 3+ consecutive periods is eligible for reinstatement of standard limit-review eligibility, even if it was previously flagged under POL-002 or POL-003. Recovery from Sub-standard/Watch back to Standard requires sustained improvement (not a single-period jump) to avoid premature relaxation of restrictions on accounts that may revert.

## Scope of reinstatement (proposed clarification)

**Reinstatement** concerns restoration of eligibility that was actually limited or restricted before the recovery. Being in the Standard band by itself is not evidence of a previous restriction. When account-level restriction or reinstatement status is unavailable, report it as unknown; do not assert that the account is restricted or that reinstatement is needed. Becoming eligible for standard limit **review** is not the same as automatic approval of a credit-limit increase.

## Relationship to warning records (proposed clarification)

The three-period improved-bin condition concerns reinstatement eligibility, not automatic closure of a warning under POL-002. Any warning-clearance decision follows the separately defined POL-002 workflow. The reference bin used to judge 'improved bin' and whether a restriction was actually imposed may require evidence absent from the score-history database; if so, identify those missing inputs rather than infer them.
