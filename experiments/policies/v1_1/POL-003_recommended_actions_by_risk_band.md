# POL-003 — Recommended Actions by Risk Band

**Version:** v1.1 experiment (synthetic project), 2026-10-10. Not approved for production use. Core band actions are unchanged; interpretive limits are working clarifications and do not authorize new limit restrictions.

## Risk-band actions (unchanged)

Prime (Bins 7–9): no action required; eligible for limit increases at standard review cycles. Standard (Bins 4–6): monitor at normal cadence; no proactive outreach unless early-warning criteria are triggered. Sub-standard/Watch (Bins 1–3): reduce or freeze credit limit increases, flag for manual underwriting on new applications, and initiate customer contact if the account entered this band within the last 2 periods (recent entrants carry higher near-term default risk than long-standing occupants of the band).

## Standard band versus limit restrictions (proposed clarification)

Being in Standard (Bins 4–6) **does not by itself establish** a restriction on credit-limit increases under this memo. It also does not guarantee approval for a limit increase. Do not recommend 'freeze all increases', 'deny increases', or 'wait until Prime' as a mandatory action solely because an account is in Standard. Determine actual eligibility from separate applicable credit-limit/underwriting rules and any documented account-specific restrictions, if available; otherwise state that eligibility cannot be determined from these policy memos alone.

## Interaction with warnings (proposed clarification)

The absence of a newly triggered warning in the latest month does not prove that a previous logged warning is closed. Assess the warning event, ongoing condition, and record status separately under POL-002. Follow documented review/escalation procedures if warning criteria are met. Do not invent an outreach, automatic limit restriction, or automatic warning-clearance rule from the Standard-band classification alone.
